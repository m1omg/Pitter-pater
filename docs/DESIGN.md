# PITTER-PATTER — design notes

A short story-driven JRPG in the spirit of RPG Maker games (Omori, Undertale, End Roll, Ib, Re:Kinder),
with original story, characters, systems, art and music.

## Premise
PIM (10) lives with her MOM and two pets, BISCUIT (grumpy orange cat) and WAFFLES (goofy golden dog).
Since DAD left, Mom has stopped getting out of bed. Pim tries very hard to be "fine": she makes breakfast,
feeds the pets, answers the phone, tells the neighbour everything is great, and never, ever cries.

Every night Pim dreams of PUDDLETON — her house blown up into a whole rainy kingdom
(the kitchen is Crumb Valley, the living room is Carpet Hills, Mom's room is the Cloud Keep).
The SUN of Puddleton has fallen asleep and it has rained for as long as anyone remembers.
In the dream Biscuit and Waffles can talk, and a gentle cloud-sheep called MOMO joins them.
Pim is sure that if she wakes the Sun, Mom will be okay again.

The truth (revealed in the Attic): when Dad left, he gave Pim his red polka-dot umbrella and said
"Be a big girl. Don't cry. Keep your mom dry for me." Pim has been holding the umbrella open ever since.
The rain is Mom's sadness — and Pim's too. An umbrella can't stop rain; it only keeps you apart from it.
In the finale Pim closes the umbrella and cries with the RAIN QUEEN (Mom's gloom). Momo — a piece of Mom's
love that followed Pim into the dream — returns to her. In the real world Pim finally says "I'm not fine",
Mom holds her, and promises to get help. The sun comes back after the rain, not instead of it.

## Core mechanic tied to theme
Battle moods: CHEERY (sun), GLOOMY (rain), HUFFY (thunder). Mixing two gives RAINBOW / STORMY / HEATWAVE,
all three gives OVERWHELMED (stun + vulnerable). Pim can NOT become GLOOMY — any gloom turns into a forced
CHEERY ("Pim smiles it away."). Only in the finale, after closing the umbrella, can she be GLOOMY and CRY.

## Structure
Prologue (dream: Pillow Fort) → Morning 1 → Night 1 (Crumb Valley, boss Grumbles the Toaster)
→ Morning 2 → Night 2 (Carpet Hills, Static Woods, boss The Static) → Evening 3 (real attic)
→ Night 3 (The Attic — Pim alone, dark, Ib/End Roll style) → Night 4 (Cloud Keep, the Rain Queen)
→ Morning 5 (ending) → credits.
